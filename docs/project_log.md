# Project log

The dated record of what was built, measured, refuted and closed in this
repository. Split out of the repo-root `project-notes.md` on **2026-08-20**, verbatim,
because `project-notes.md` is gitignored (`.gitignore:239`) and so this history had no
version control and no undo. It now has both.

`project-notes.md` answers *what is true now*; this file answers *when we learned it,
and what we believed before*. published published guidance for writing a
context file lists "changelogs or history" among the things to leave out of it,
which is why the two are separated rather than merged.

## How to use this file

* **Entries are dated and appended. They are never rewritten.** An entry states
  what was true and believed on its date. When a later result overtakes it, the
  original text stays and a **bracketed, dated marker** is inserted beside the
  affected number, pointing at what replaced it and at the artifact that settles
  it. Deleting the old value would destroy the record of when the project
  believed it; leaving it unmarked is what made a 2026-08-05 thesis check
  re-derive two superseded Stage 6.6 component figures from scratch.
* **This file is not authoritative for any current number.** Where an entry and
  a document of record differ, the document of record governs:
  `docs/architecture.md` (the implementation spec), the accepted ADRs in
  `docs/decisions/`, and the four reports (`phase2_report.md`,
  `phase3_report.md`, `stage6_6_report.md`,
  `production_campaign_2026-07-29.md`) **including their dated addenda, which
  are authoritative where they differ from their own bodies**.
* **Grammar.** This is an undated `docs/<name>.md`, i.e. a document of record
  under `docs/conventions.md` section 9.1, kept current by appending dated
  sections rather than by editing the body. That is the same pattern as
  `phase2_report.md` sections 11 to 14.

Adding an entry: one bullet per closed piece of work, newest last, dated
`YYYY-MM-DD`, naming its driver, its evidence artifact and its gates. The
mechanics are in the `bep-docs-and-writing` guide.
